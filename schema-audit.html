Approach

Root cause: 22 pages use SoftwareApplication schema without aggregateRating or review — Google requires one of these. Semrush flags this as an invalid structured data item.

Fix: Replace SoftwareApplication with Service. Service has no rating requirement, fits SaaS offerings naturally, and matches the pattern already used on /solutions/cortexeye.
