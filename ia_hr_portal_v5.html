Who We Are

Impact Analytics is an Agentic AI company — we build AI-native software that helps the world's leading retailers and consumer brands plan smarter, price better, and run leaner. Founded in 2015, headquartered in Atlanta, GA, with our India operations based in Bangalore.

We have 150+ clients across 20+ countries, have delivered over $2B+ in value, and are trusted by some of the world's most recognised brands — Victoria's Secret, Under Armour, Levi's, Coach, Ralph Lauren, Dollar General, Patagonia, and 100+ more. Our mission: Clarity From Chaos With Agentic AI.

But more than the tech, it's the people that make IA what it is. A team of builders, thinkers, and doers who genuinely care — about the work, about customers, and about each other.

Our Culture & What We Stand For
Impact is everything We don't measure success by how busy we are — we measure it by the difference we make. You'll always know why your work matters here.
You'll never work alone Collaboration isn't a buzzword here — it's how we actually operate. Your teammates will show up for you, and you'll have the chance to show up for them.
Your ideas will be heard We don't believe great ideas only come from the top. Whether you're on Day 1 or Year 5, your perspective has a seat at the table.
You'll grow faster than you expect We invest in our people — through learning programs, real stretch opportunities, honest feedback, and managers who genuinely care about your career.
We take the work seriously — not ourselves High standards and good humour coexist just fine at IA. We work hard, celebrate wins, and make room for the human side of work.

HR Systems Walkthrough Onboarding · First Week
Keka — your HR system, explained.
The HR systems walkthrough is conducted on Day 3 by your HRBP. Here is a guide to everything you need to do on Keka from Day 1 onwards.
Attendance
In-office days — Your Tap In/Out card automatically syncs with Keka. Always tap in when you arrive and tap out when you leave.
WFH days — Mark attendance manually on Keka before 10 AM. Go to Attendance → Mark Attendance → Work From Home.
Missed attendance — Raise a regularisation request on Keka within 3 days. Manager approval is required.
Leave Management
Applying for leave — On Keka, click the Me icon → Leave → Apply Leave. Select leave type, dates, and add a note. Submit for manager approval.
Leave balance — Click Me → Leave → Leave Balance to view your available days. Balances reset annually.
Leave types — Earned Leave, Sick Leave, Casual Leave, Maternity/Paternity Leave. Full policy available under Me → Leave → Leave Policy.
Payroll — greytHR
IA uses greytHR as its payroll platform — separate from Keka. Access to greytHR is set up by the Operations team, who will share your login credentials and walk you through the platform during your first week.
Payslips — Available on greytHR every month. Log in → go to Payroll → My Payslips to view and download as PDF.
Form 16 — Issued annually on greytHR after the financial year close under Tax → Form 16.
Access — Credentials and platform walkthrough provided by the Operations team during your first week onboarding.
Provident Fund (EPF) — Action required on Day 1
Both you and IA each contribute 12% of basic salary to EPF every month. Your UAN is your permanent PF identity number.

Activate your UAN at the EPFO member portal using Aadhaar, PAN, or Member ID
Link Aadhaar to UAN — Go to Manage → KYC in the EPFO portal — mandatory for all transactions

The 30-60-90 plan is a structured guide to help you ramp up confidently. It is built with your manager and reviewed with HR at key milestones. Think of it as your personal roadmap — not a performance measure.

Foundation phase

Your Data at IA
IA collects personal data including your contact details, bank information, tax details, health records (for insurance), and employment history for HR and payroll administration
Your data is handled in accordance with applicable Indian data protection laws. IA's privacy policy is available at impactanalytics.co/privacy-policy
You have the right to access, review, and request correction of your personal data. Write to HR at hr-india@impactanalytics.co
Your Obligations as an Employee
Handle all client and employee data only for its intended business purpose — never for personal gain
Never share confidential or proprietary information with unauthorised parties — including via AI tools, personal email, or social media
Lock your screen, secure your device, and never leave documents with confidential information visible on your desk or screen
Do not insert personal USB drives or unauthorised hardware into company devices. Use only IA-authorised storage
On leaving IA, return all confidential information and devices. Your confidentiality obligations continue even after employment ends
Data breach? If you suspect unauthorised access to company or client data, report it to IT immediately at it-team@impactanalytics.co and notify your manager. Do not attempt to resolve it on your own.
Statutory Training Onboarding · Compliance
Mandatory training — required by law and by IA.
Certain training programmes are legally mandated for all employees. These must be completed within your first 30 days and refreshed annually.

Mandatory under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013. Covers definitions, examples, reporting channels, and ICC. Completed in the first week. To file a complaint, submit in writing to the ICC within 3 months of the incident.

Confidentiality & Data Privacy Training
Practical guidance on handling client and employee data, NDA obligations, and what constitutes a data breach.
Anti-Bribery & Ethics Awareness
IA's zero-tolerance approach to corruption, gifts policy, and obligations under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.
Fire Safety & Emergency Evacuation

Deadline: All statutory training must be completed within 30 days of joining. HR will track completion and follow up. Failure to complete is a compliance violation. Reach out to hr-india@impactanalytics.co with any questions.
Background Verification Onboarding · Compliance
A standard part of joining IA.
Background verification is conducted by IA's authorised third-party BGV vendor for all new joiners. It is initiated after Day 1 once your documents are submitted on Keka.
What Gets Verified
Identity verification — Aadhaar, PAN card, and any government-issued ID submitted at joining
Employment history — Previous employers, designations, dates of employment, and relieving status
Educational qualifications — Degree certificates (10th, 12th, graduation) verified with the issuing institution
Process & Timeline
BGV is initiated after Day 1 once all your documents are uploaded on Keka
The BGV vendor will contact you directly via email to collect consent and any additional information required
The process typically takes 2–4 weeks. Please respond promptly to any requests from the vendor to avoid delays
Results are shared confidentially with IA's HR team
Important: Any false, inaccurate, or misleading information provided at joining — including in documents submitted for BGV — may result in withdrawal of the employment offer or termination of employment, as per the IA Employee Handbook. Ensure all details submitted are accurate.
Questions? Contact HR at hr-india@impactanalytics.co. For BGV-specific queries, respond directly to the vendor's communication.
